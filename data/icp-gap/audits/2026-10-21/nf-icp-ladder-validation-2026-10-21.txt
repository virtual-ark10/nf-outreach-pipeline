ICYpeas   71/100  (24 fit, 21 gap, 17 integration, 9 commercial)  Evidence High
  site: https://www.icypeas.com/
  product: French contact-discovery API: email finder, catch-all verifier, reverse lookup, domain
  scan and profile scrapers, sold on credits to sales teams and platforms that embed enrichment.
  verified gap: their scope is person and domain contact records. No publication index, article
  corpus, sponsor mentions or momentum, so they cannot answer which newsletters wrote about a
  company.
  evidence: "Discover valid emails (including catchalls), and scrape contacts at scale."; "Our
  billion-plus record database is fully refreshed every month"; "80% of our customers embed the
  Icypeas API in their own product or automated workflows."
  why second wave: real API surface, but a small credits-based vendor, so a data licence would be a
  new cost line for them. Contact: contact@icypeas.com (published).

KADOA   66/100  (22 fit, 21 gap, 15 integration, 8 commercial)  Evidence Medium
  site: https://www.kadoa.com/
  product: web data extraction and monitoring for investment firms, delivering to Snowflake, S3,
  BigQuery, spreadsheets, webhooks, MCP and API.
  verified gap: ingestion is URL-scoped pages and documents only (HTML, XML, RSS, JSON, PDF). No
  newsletter corpus, no cross-publication entity or sponsor extraction, no activity tracking.
  evidence: "A workflow's source is the set of URLs it extracts from."; "Our agents build, monitor,
  and maintain mission-critical web data pipelines for investment teams and AI agents."
  why second wave: partly supply-side. Their funds would benefit from a newsletter corpus, but the
  realistic deal is licensed input, and they could hand-build newsletter scraping.

HIREEZ   47/100  (12 fit, 17 gap, 12 integration, 6 commercial)  Evidence Low
  site: https://hireez.com/
  gap: sourcing is enumerated as ATS, job boards, open web and partner databases; newsletters are
  not a source. But they need candidate profiles and resumes, which NF does not produce, and they
  already run 1B+ profiles of their own. Data-partner conversation at best, not a buyer.

ZYTE   45/100  (15 fit, 9 gap, 15 integration, 6 commercial)  Evidence Medium
  site: https://www.zyte.com/
  gap: unverified. They sell extraction capacity per URL and already auto-extract article content,
  so a newsletter corpus is raw material their managed service could build. Supply-side.

ORBIFOLD AI   26/100  (8 fit, 6 gap, 9 integration, 3 commercial)  Evidence Low
  site: https://www.orbifold.ai/
  gap: unverified. They sell multimodal data infrastructure for physical AI, on customer-tenant
  data. No newsletter, publication or company-intelligence use case exists to attach to.
